About me

I'm an industrial engineer from Universidad de La Sabana, where I also earned an M.S. in Applied Analytics with distinction under a full research fellowship, and I hold a Graduate Certificate in Logistics and Supply Chain Management from MIT. I currently teach business analytics and optimization at Universidad Externado de Colombia.

In more than four years of practice, I have managed over USD 150,000 a month in paid media for companies in the United States, Brazil and Colombia, led data science projects for multinational companies in banking, consumer goods and telecommunications, and trained more than 100 professionals. On the research side, that work has led to a peer-reviewed international publication and several working papers on location and logistics problems.

I am also an expert in artificial intelligence: I bring machine learning and generative AI into research, growth, data and training, to move faster without giving up rigor.

In everything I do, the same approach: listen carefully, measure honestly and keep learning from each result.
