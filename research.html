Research & Teaching

Optimization models, tested on real data.

My research formulates hard optimization problems, from wildfire response to urban food access and air cargo, and designs the algorithms to solve them.

Research lines

Wildfire and emergency logistics

Joint location of aerial suppression bases and water refill points under uncertainty, with two-stage stochastic programming, CVaR and matheuristics.

Facility location and food access

Competitive and public location models that combine MINLP, discrete choice modeling and Fuzzy AHP to place markets and stores where communities need them.

Air cargo and supply chains

Integer programming and online 3D bin packing to load cut flower charter flights more efficiently, in collaboration with MIT's Center for Transportation and Logistics.
